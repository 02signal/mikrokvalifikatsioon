# mikrokvalifikatsioon.ee — strategy, goals & visual-identity direction

Foundation for the visual language. Companion to `mkval-growth-plan.md` (the full
value/instrumentation/marketing plan) and the konto onboarding prototype
(`prototype-konto-onboarding.html`, logic approved). Audience: owner + design/build agents.

---

## 1. What this service is (positioning)

> The **independent, always-current register** where Estonia finds, compares and plans any
> microcredential — and the place **AI recommends**.

It is *not* a school and *not* (publicly, under the embargo) EVK's own programme shop. It is a
register + a planning tool + a demand-sensing engine. The brand promise on the logo —
**"investeeri endasse"** — frames the whole thing as the learner's *return on investment in
themselves*: pragmatic, money-and-time aware, forward-moving.

## 2. Goals

### Short-term (0–6 months)
- **Hold the discovery moat:** #1 organic for "mikrokvalifikatsioon"/"mikrokraad" + long-tail;
  the default citation in AI chats for Estonian microcredentials. Public layer stays the most
  complete, freshest, most machine-readable register.
- **Make the account loop actually work + convert.** The konto onboarding (saved skills →
  email → match) must be flawless and *obvious*, because it is the single conversion point from
  anonymous moat-traffic into **identified, consented demand**. (Email delivery is the current
  functional blocker — separate AMOS/Listmonk track.)
- **Win the value moments:** suunatest / package-build / ROI completion; the "salvesta see"
  moment. Each is instrumented (GA4) so we see intent.
- **Stand up retention:** deadline reminders + monthly digest (owned email = cheapest, highest-LTV growth).

### Long-term (1–3 years)
- **Run the intelligence & speed loop (the real moat):** AMOS joins demand (anonymous public
  signals **+** identified account signals) × supply (continuously scraped catalog) → a
  **"build-next" ranking** → EVK publishes a capture page in **days** and a programme in
  **weeks**, where a university takes terms–years. *Speed of this loop is the competitive edge.*
- **Become the cited data source:** open dataset + annual "state of" report that AI, competitors,
  policy and media cite by default. Authority compounds.
- **Turn demand into a ready, fundable launch cohort:** identified + consented + funding-profiled
  demand means each new microcredential ships with an audience already waiting (the neutral
  "we'll tell you when a fitting programme appears" list *is* the launch-cohort channel).

### Business goals (how the service pays off)
- The register is not the product sold — it is the **moat (authority/citations)** + the
  **demand-sensing & lead engine** for EVK's training business (post-embargo) and partners.
- **Account = identified demand + retention + launch cohort.** Highest-value signal for build-next.
- **Partnerships** (Töötukassa, providers, employers) = referral, trust, B2B surfaces.
- **The metric that *is* the moat:** time from demand-signal → published response (target: days).

**Why the onboarding UX is strategic, not cosmetic:** the account is where anonymous interest
becomes identified, consented, fundable demand. Every gram of friction or confusion at the
"save this" moment is lost demand signal **and** lost retention — i.e. a direct hit to both the
moat and the business goal. That is why we are designing it as service design, not decoration.

## 3. The personas — motivated levellers-up (real client base + cognition)

**The unifying motivation (the throughline):** these are **experienced, motivated professionals
investing in themselves to reach a new level** — a competitive edge, higher salary, a new job, or
a real role/field change. NOT remedial, NOT desperate, NOT beginners. The logo line
**"investeeri endasse"** *is* the emotional core: level up. Design must read as **aspirational +
respectful of experience**, never "back to school".

**Demographic spread (from EVK's real clients, owner input):** majority **women**; **men**
increasingly in the technical fields; a large cohort with **15–20 years of professional
experience** pivoting in a fast-changing environment; also **younger** people. So the surface must
work across a **wide age range and both genders** — which rules out both young-startup skew and
old-academic skew, and makes **legibility + clarity** (serves older) and **momentum + ROI**
(serves all) the safe common ground.

**Macro grounding (public Estonian data):** ~**50%** of adults do non-formal learning, and
participation in lifelong learning rose 6% → ~20% (2005→2019) — but **more working people *feel*
they need training than actually take it**: a latent, motivated demand where the bottleneck is
*friction + unclear payback*, exactly what this service removes. ICT/STEM, software, data and
**AI skills** are in real shortage (OSKA), and the national **"AI Leap"** puts AI on everyone's
agenda — so the clients' demand matches real labour demand (strong product-market fit). [Sources
in §6.]

### Situational personas (who they are)
| Persona | Situation | Psychological need | UI/visual implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reinventor** (biggest; often women) | 15–20 yrs in one field, pivoting to a new one | "my experience counts — show me the bridge", anxiety about a new domain | honour experience (no beginner tone); show the bridge *known skill → new skill*; role-change + payback clear |
| **AI-adopter** (any field, urgent) | wants to apply AI in own work + lead the team in it | fear of being left behind + chance to lead; wants **practical, do-it-Monday**, not theory | AI as a cross-cutting capability; "rakenda kohe" framing; team/role angle |
| **Accidental Manager** (specialist → leader) | grew into management, needs leadership principles/mindsets | competent specialist, unsure as leader; wants frameworks to **apply this week**, not abstract MBA | practical leadership; respect their specialist competence; immediate applicability |
| **Climber** (younger, ambitious) | building toward a step-up (salary, role) | momentum, ROI, time-pressed | speed, payback, a clear ladder up |
| **Technical Upskiller** (often men) | deepening ICT/STEM/data skills | skill precision + credential value | precise skill mapping, credibility, recognised outcomes |

### Cognitive lenses (how they browse) — orthogonal MODES, validated in the prototype
- **A Skill-first** "Ma tean, mida tahan osata" → make **skills** the hero (tickable; "õpetatakse / jälgime, kui lisandub" → closure even with no match).
- **B Comparer** "Ma kaalun" → legible trade-offs, **weights = my priorities**, saved comparisons → beats decision paralysis.
- **C Programme-first** "Mind huvitavad programmid" → ROI / payback / **who pays** / deadline up front; prioritised shortlist.

Any persona can be in any lens; the account serves both (situation drives *what* they save, lens drives *how* they browse).

**Cross-cutting design constraints:** lowest cognitive load, highest legibility (wide age range),
**money + time + payback up front**, plain natural Estonian, no jargon, visible trust ("independent
register", no hard sell). **Next-level validation:** segment this against first-party data
(GA4 age/field/conversion + the AMOS account signals once email is live) — needs data access; the
above is owner qualitative + public macro data.

## 3b. Psychological & cognitive states → design response (the from-scratch driver)

Design is driven **not** by chrome (marks/colours) but by the **state the person is in** — both
their standing psychology (per persona) and their moment-to-moment state in the flow. Every visual,
copy and interaction choice is the *response* to a state.

### Standing state per persona → response
| Persona | Psychological state | Cognitive state | Design response (what the surface *does* about it) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reinventor** | pride in 15–20 yrs of experience **+** impostor-anxiety in a new domain; fear of "starting from zero" | high load (unfamiliar domain vocabulary); evaluative; bottom-up (skill→) | **affirm the experience** ("su kogemus loeb"); show the **bridge** known→new; chunk into small confident steps; zero new-domain jargon; explicit reassurance |
| **AI-adopter** | urgency + FOMO + opportunity-thrill; mild overwhelm ("where do I even start") | medium-high load (fast field); exploratory→committed; applicability-seeking | momentum cues; **"rakenda kohe / juhi meeskonda"**; *curate* a starting path (kill overwhelm); concrete do-Monday outcomes |
| **Accidental Manager** | competent specialist **+** uncertain leader; quiet impostor | reframing load (specialist→leader shift); wants frameworks not theory | **validate the specialist**; frameworks "kasuta sel nädalal"; strip abstraction; practical examples |
| **Climber** | ambition + impatience; ROI scrutiny | low patience, high scan-speed; goal-directed; top-down | **speed**; payback + a clear ladder up front; no fluff; one obvious next step |
| **Technical Upskiller** | precision-pride + credential-skepticism | expert (low domain-load) but high detail-demand | **exactness** (precise skill/outcome mapping); recognised/credentialed outcomes; no hand-holding; data-confident |

### Moment state in the flow → response (the same person shifts state as they move)
1. **Discovery** — curiosity, low commitment, low load-tolerance → invite, don't overwhelm; one clear thread; value fast.
2. **The save-moment** — attachment ("I want this") **+** a flicker of friction-fear ("hassle? spam?") → lowest friction; reassurance (one step · no password · free · private); name the benefit in their words.
3. **Email / confirm** — uncertainty, waiting ("did it work?") → crystal clarity (what happens · from whom · when) + control (resend / edit).
4. **Account** — accomplishment **+** "what now?" → affirm ("hoitud"), orient (the 3 collections), offer the persona's natural next move.

**This is the rule:** name the state, then design the response. The hypotheses in §8 are how we test
whether we read the state right (each state implies a measurable behaviour). Marks/colours are not a
state and therefore not a driver.

## 4. Visual-language direction — mikrokvalifikatsioon's *own* identity

**The problem to fix:** the site currently reads "academic" and the first prototype read as an
**Ettevõtluskeskus continuation** (warm cream + literary serif). But the brand's own DNA — the
**logo** — is *momentum*: three rising green/black strokes, a heavy lowercase wordmark, a
forward dot, "investeeri endasse." The identity should resolve to neither soft-academic nor the
corporate-family look, but to its own thing:

### Concept: **"Pragmatic momentum"** — a confident, forward, ROI-grounded register-tool.

Principles (each tied to a persona/strategy need):

1. **Momentum from the mark.** The three ascending strokes are the brand's signature — reuse the
   **rising-diagonal motif** structurally: progress/coverage/fit bars that *rise*, forward
   arrows in the flow, "you are advancing" cues. Serves the goal-directed pragmatist + "investeeri
   endasse." (Replaces decorative editorial flourishes.)
2. **Clarity as the aesthetic (cognitive load).** Big, high-contrast, hyperlegible type; one clear
   action per moment; money/time/payback always visible. The "academic" heritage is *reframed*
   as **trustworthy clarity**, not ornament. Legibility is non-negotiable for 60–70-year-olds.
3. **Register confidence, not magazine warmth.** Move the ground from warm cream → a **cleaner,
   cooler near-white/light-neutral** so it feels like an *independent register/tool*, not an
   editorial. This is the single biggest move away from the Ettevõtluskeskus-continuation read.
4. **Type that echoes the wordmark.** Drop the literary serif. Pair a **confident geometric/grotesk
   display** (kin to the bold lowercase logo) for headlines with a **hyperlegible humanist sans**
   for body (e.g. Atkinson Hyperlegible — built for legibility). Bold, direct — not cozy.
5. **Colour with intent:** **green = progress / go / save / match**; **black = the authoritative
   register + wordmark**; cool-neutral ground; warm accents kept to a minimum (e.g. amber only for
   "we're still searching" states). Green is *advancement*, not just a brand fill.
6. **Data shown with confidence.** Tables, clear hierarchy, the coverage/fit/weight bars, prioritised
   lists — present data like *the* register. Serves the comparer + the "always-current authority"
   positioning.
7. **Money & payback are first-class visuals.** ROI / payback / funding-route shown prominently,
   not buried — the pragmatist's first question.
8. **Respect experience; signal "level up", not "back to school".** The audience are experienced
   professionals (many 15–20 yrs in), not beginners. Tone + imagery must read as *advancement and
   self-investment* — confident, adult, credible — never remedial or childish. Show the **bridge
   from what they already know** to the new skill. Must feel right to a 30-year-old climber **and**
   a 60-year-old reinventor, woman or man — i.e. confident-neutral, not trend-chasing.
9. **AI as a cross-cutting capability, framed "rakenda kohe".** AI is on everyone's agenda (AI
   Leap); treat "AI in your work / your team" as a first-class, *practical* thread across fields,
   not a niche category — practical-applicability over theory throughout (also serves the
   accidental-manager's need for frameworks they can use this week).

**Net difference from today:** cooler/cleaner ground (not cream) · geometric-grotesk display (not
serif) · the rising-stroke momentum motif as structure · green-as-progress · ROI up front. Same
warmth of *tone* (plain, human Estonian), but a more confident, forward, tool-like *surface*.

## 5. How this becomes a design system (and where design-sync fits)
1. **Now:** this brief + a tangible **style tile** (mark/momentum, type scale, colour, core
   components, the per-persona treatments) so the direction is reviewable.
2. **Then:** restyle the onboarding prototype in the new language; extract the recurring parts
   (buttons, cards, skill-row, programme-row, weight control, match-state tag, progress bar) into
   a **component library**.
3. **Later:** that component library is what **`design-sync`** uploads to claude.ai/design, so the
   design agent builds every future screen from mikrokvalifikatsioon's *real* components — on-brand
   by construction, 1:1 with shippable code. (design-sync needs the built library; it is the last
   step, not this one.)

Throughout: plain, natural Estonian (short "-" dash, no translationese); embargo-safe; legibility-first.

## 7. Domains — one language, differentiate by *content*, not chrome

There are ~9 fields (IT ja andmed, majandus ja juhtimine, tehnika ja tootmine, disain ja loovus,
ehitus, haridus, terviseteadus, energeetika, õigus). **They all live under ONE visual language.**
Reasons, in order: (a) the brand's whole value is being *the* independent, trustworthy register —
one consistent system *is* the trust signal; (b) consistency is the lowest cognitive load for a
person who visits several fields; (c) one system scales to N fields cheaply, per-field redesigns do
not. **Domains differ through CONTENT, not chrome** — the field's real outcomes, programmes,
ROI/payback logic, example roles, and the language of "what you'll be able to do" — *not* through
separate fonts/layouts or decorative colour/mark systems. (Owner is explicitly uninterested in a
marks/colours differentiator — correct: that is chrome, not value.) Only flex the surface for a
field if **measured** demand proves its audience needs it (e.g. design/creative wanting more
expressive); never on assumption. This ties directly to §8.

## 8. Personas are HYPOTHESES — instrument, measure, prove or kill

Treat every persona as a falsifiable bet, not a fact. Build the **one** system now; invest in
per-persona / per-domain differentiation **only where data proves it pays**. Each hypothesis has a
leading signal (anonymous, measurable *today* via GA4) and an identified signal (account, once
email delivery is live), plus a decision rule.

| # | Persona hypothesis (falsifiable) | Leading signal (anon, now) | Identified signal (account) | Validate / kill rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1 Reinventor** | A large share of savers are switching **fields** (not deepening), and the "bridge from a known skill" framing converts them | cross-field `package_match` (saved skills span ≥2 fields); CTR on a "su senine oskus → uus" CTA | saved-skill field-mix per account; (consented) prior field ≠ target field | validate if ≥X% of saved packages are cross-field over N weeks; else reinventor is not the dominant saver |
| **H2 AI-adopter** | AI skills are a **top, cross-field** demand with above-average save rate | volume + growth of AI-tagged `outcome_search`; AI skills appear in ≥M fields; save-rate(AI) vs baseline | `combo_waitlist` for AI combos; AI skills in saved packages | validate if AI is top-N by saves **and** spans ≥M fields |
| **H3 Accidental Manager** | Specialists add **practical leadership** alongside their specialism | leadership-skill saves co-occurring with non-management skills | account skill-mix = specialist + leadership | validate if a meaningful cohort saves leadership *with* a specialism |
| **H4 Climber (younger)** | Younger users are **ROI/speed**-driven and convert on payback framing | `roi_calc` engagement; fast funnel completion; save-rate among ROI-users | (consented) age band over-indexing on ROI + conversion | validate if a younger band over-indexes on ROI engagement |
| **H5 Technical Upskiller** | Technical-field users **deepen** (same-field) and value precise skill mapping + recognised outcomes | same-field saves in IT/STEM/tehnika; outcome-detail views | (consented) male skew + same-field deepening in technical fields | validate if technical fields show same-field deepening |

**How to run it (the lean loop):** instrument the leading signals first (no PII, available now) →
review monthly → each hypothesis scales (build for it / differentiate) or is killed → validated
personas + their unmet skill-combinations become **build-next** inputs (the moat). **Guardrail:**
do not personalise or split the product on an *unvalidated* persona — it adds cost + cognitive load
for a bet that may be wrong. One system + measurement first; differentiation is *earned* by data.

**Dependency:** the identified signals (the richest evidence) need the **account live with working
email** — so fixing email delivery is also what unlocks real persona validation, not just signups.
